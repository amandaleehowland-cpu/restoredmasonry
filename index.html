Common questions

Questions homeowners ask

How do I know if my masonry needs repointing?

Look for mortar you can scratch out with a key, gaps between brick, white powdery staining, or brick faces flaking off. Any of those means water is getting in. A site visit will tell you how far it has progressed.

Why does the mortar type matter on an older building?

Most buildings put up before roughly 1930 were laid with soft lime mortar. Modern Portland cement is harder than the brick around it, so it forces moisture and movement into the masonry units instead of the joints. That is what causes spalling. The mortar has to be matched to the original.

Do I need a heritage permit?

If your property is designated or sits in a heritage conservation district, likely yes. Municipal heritage staff are part of the process on many of these projects and I can help you through the application.

What time of year can the work be done?

Lime mortar needs temperatures above roughly 5 °C to cure properly, so the season runs spring through late fall in eastern Ontario. Booking early in the year gets you a better spot in the schedule.

How long does a project take?

A chimney rebuild or a small repointing area is often a few days. A full facade can run several weeks. Your proposal gives you a firm window before anything starts.
